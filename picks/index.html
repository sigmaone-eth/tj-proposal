Pairs are made in All photos. Tap + Selected Work on a photo and it becomes the left side of Pair 1. The next photo you pick fills the right side of Pair 1, the one after that starts Pair 2, and so on. Pairs show here top to bottom, in the order visitors scroll. Use ⇄ Swap to switch a pair's left and right, and × to take a photo out.

To change the order: grab a pair by ⠿ Move and drag it up or down (on a phone, hold and drag with your finger). Put your favorite at the top. You can also tap ↑ or ↓ to move a pair one spot.
